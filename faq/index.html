Straight answers

Questions Families Actually Ask

Prices, delivery, sanitization, toddlers, weather and the food truck combo - answered honestly.

How much does a bounce house rental cost?

8-hour rentals: Tiny Town toddler units $85-$100, theme bounce houses $150-$175, bounce and slide combos $175-$275, water and dry slides $225-$450. The Foam Ball BLASTER party is $225 for the first 2 hours and $100 each extra hour.

What areas do you deliver to?

Delivery is free in Pueblo. Pueblo West, Colorado Springs, Fountain, Security-Widefield, Canon City, Florence and Penrose are $50 (up to 50 miles). 51-75 miles is $75, 76-100 miles is $100. Beyond 100 miles we negotiate - just ask.

Are the inflatables cleaned?

Yes - every inflatable is sanitized before AND after each rental, every time.

How long do we get the unit?

Standard rentals are a full 8-hour day. The Foam Ball BLASTER party runs 2 hours of hosted play, $100 per extra hour.

Can toddlers use the big units?

Tiny Town is built for ages 5 and under (each child 100 lbs or less on the duel units). Call us with your kids' ages and we will point you to the right unit.

How far ahead should I book?

Popular weekends go first, especially in summer. Call or text 719-299-0772 to check your date - we will tell you honestly what is open.

What happens if the weather turns?

Southern Colorado weather changes fast. Ask about rescheduling when you book and we will work with you.

How does the food truck combo work?

Pair any 8-hour inflatable with a local food truck booked through whereeatin.com (their $250 catering deposit is credited to your food bill) and take $25 OFF the inflatable, or half off the $50 delivery to the Springs area. Food trucks listed on Where We Eatin' get 20% OFF weekday and pop-up inflatables plus a $20 referral credit per party booked.

What do you need from us for setup?

A flat spot for the unit, a path to reach it, and power for the blower. We confirm the exact needs for your address when you book.

Do you do school, church and fundraiser events?

Yes - obstacle courses, game parties and big slides are favorites for carnivals and field days. Call for group and multi-unit rates.
